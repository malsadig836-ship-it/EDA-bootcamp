TensorOrbit EDA Bootcamp - Capstone Datasets

Students may choose any dataset and decide their own business questions, cleaning steps, visualisations, findings, and recommendations.

Each dataset intentionally contains a few missing values, duplicate rows, and unusual values/outliers for EDA practice.

Datasets:
1. Supermarket Sales - 153 rows - Explore branch, category, customer type, sales and profit performance.
2. Cafe Orders - 143 rows - Explore popular items, order channels, preparation time, ratings and customer groups.
3. Hotel Bookings - 133 rows - Explore room types, locations, revenue, booking channels and cancellations.
4. E-commerce Orders - 163 rows - Explore categories, customer segments, discounts, shipping, returns and profit.
5. Food Delivery - 148 rows - Explore delivery time, distance, weather, rider experience, ratings and late deliveries.
6. Gym Memberships - 138 rows - Explore plans, visits, satisfaction, renewal, branches and referral sources.
