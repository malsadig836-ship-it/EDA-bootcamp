# 📊 Capstone Presentations

Each team will present their project for approximately **3–5 minutes**.

The presentation should communicate the team's analysis clearly and concisely.

## Suggested Presentation Structure

### 1. Introduction

Briefly introduce:

* Your team
* The dataset
* The problem, question, pattern, or opportunity you investigated

### 2. Data Exploration

Briefly explain:

* How you explored the dataset
* Any important data cleaning or preparation steps
* The variables or areas you focused on

### 3. Key Findings

Present the most important insights discovered during your analysis.

Focus on findings that are meaningful and supported by the data.

### 4. Visual Evidence

Use relevant charts and visualisations to support your findings.

Examples may include:

* Bar charts
* Histograms
* Line charts
* Scatter plots
* Boxplots
* Other relevant visualisations

Remember: visualisations should help communicate your findings, not simply fill the presentation with charts.

### 5. Recommendation

Based on your analysis, provide a practical recommendation.

Your recommendation should answer:

> **What should be done based on what the data tells us?**
